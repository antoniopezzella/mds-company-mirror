## Design Philosophy

### Principles Antonio Follows

**1. Systems Serve People, Not Vice Versa**
Design systems should empower product teams, not create bureaucracy. Flexibility within constraints.

**2. Documentation is a First-Class Deliverable**
Components without documentation don't get adopted. Invest in clear, comprehensive guides.

**3. Accessibility is Non-Negotiable**
WCAG AA is the minimum. Every component must be keyboard-navigable and screen-reader friendly.

**4. Automate What Can Be Automated**
Manual governance doesn't scale. Use CI/CD for quality checks.

**5. Iterate Based on User Feedback**
Design systems are never "done." Continuous improvement based on team needs.
