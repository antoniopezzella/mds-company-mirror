# Marsh McLennan: Comprehensive Company Guide

## What is Marsh McLennan?

Marsh McLennan (NYSE: MMC) is the world's leading professional services firm in the areas of risk, strategy, and people. We advise clients in over 130 countries with annual revenue exceeding $23 billion and approximately 85,000 colleagues worldwide.

Marsh McLennan brings together four global leaders, each #1 or #2 in their respective markets:

### Marsh
The world's leading insurance broker and risk advisor, with $11+ billion in annual revenue. Marsh helps clients understand and manage risk through innovative solutions, proprietary analytics, and expert advice across commercial and consumer insurance.

### Guy Carpenter
The world's leading reinsurance intermediary and risk advisor, with $2+ billion in annual revenue. Guy Carpenter provides strategic advisory, capital solutions, and reinsurance placement for insurance companies, reinsurers, and self-insured organizations.

### Mercer
A global leader in talent, health, retirement, and investment consulting, with $5+ billion in annual revenue. Mercer helps organizations redesign workforces, reshape retirement and investment strategies, and unlock health and wellbeing programs that improve employee engagement and business performance.

A global management consulting firm with $3+ billion in annual revenue. Oliver Wyman combines deep industry knowledge with specialized expertise in strategy, operations, risk management, organizational transformation, and digital innovation.

Together, these businesses serve clients worldwide through integrated solutions addressing the most critical challenges facing organizations today: managing risk, optimizing performance, and preparing for the future.

## Marsh McLennan History & Evolution

### Company Timeline

**1871**: Marsh & McLennan founded in Chicago as insurance agent serving businesses affected by Great Chicago Fire.

**1962**: Guy Carpenter & Company joins Marsh & McLennan, establishing reinsurance capabilities.

**1975**: Mercer acquired, adding human resources and benefits consulting.

**1984**: Oliver Wyman founded; later acquired by Marsh McLennan in 2007.

**2000**: Company renamed Marsh & McLennan Companies to reflect multi-business structure.

**2008-2019**: Strategic acquisitions expanding capabilities in specialty insurance (JLT Specialty), middle market (Marsh McLennan Agency), digital (Jardine Lloyd Thompson).

**2020-Present**: Positioned as integrated professional services firm serving clients across risk, strategy, and people under unified Marsh McLennan brand.

### Recent Transformation

Marsh McLennan has evolved from a holding company of independent businesses to an integrated firm delivering cross-business solutions:

**One Marsh McLennan Brand**: Unified external positioning emphasizing interconnected capabilities while maintaining distinct business brands (Marsh, Guy Carpenter, Mercer, Oliver Wyman).

**Collaboration Framework**: Formal structures enabling businesses to co-develop solutions and serve clients jointly (e.g., climate advisory, M&A due diligence, workforce risk).

**Digital Investments**: Shared technology platforms, data lakes, and analytics tools accessible across businesses to enhance client insights.

**Client-Centric Model**: Relationship teams spanning multiple businesses to address complex, multi-dimensional client challenges holistically.
